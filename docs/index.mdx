## What Drone Capability is

A datasheet gives TOPS and watts. A drone designer needs to know whether a given algorithm on a given part lets the drone fly 15 m/s through a forest, and what it costs in endurance. This repo connects the two: a model of how compute power, mass and latency change a mission's speed, range and endurance, and a harness that measures those three numbers on real flight computers.
